The VELORA Philosophy

Heritage Craft,
Made for Today

VELORA began with a simple belief: that Indian ethnic wear should be a celebration of craft, not a fleeting trend. Every piece in our studio is designed to honour the weaver's hand — from Banarasi silks and Kanjivarams to zardozi-embroidered lehengas and hand-stitched juttis.

We work directly with handloom clusters in Varanasi, Kanchipuram and Patan, and with embroidery artisans across Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh. By sourcing responsibly and paying fair wages, we help preserve centuries-old techniques while creating timeless pieces for the modern wardrobe.
